Metropolitan train

Getting to Flemington Racecourse

Trains will operate from Flinders Street (Platform 6) stopping only at Southern Cross (Platform 11) and North Melbourne (Platform 4), enroute to Flemington Racecourse (Platform 1).

  • From 3:40pm to 5:40pm, services will depart every 20 minutes.
  • From 5:40pm to 7:10pm, services will depart every 30 minutes.
  • The last departure from Flinders Street will be at 7.10pm

Trains returning from Flemington Racecourse will stop at North Melbourne (Platform 3) and Southern Cross (Platform 12) on approach to Flinders Street.

Leaving from Flemington Racecourse 

Trains will operate from Flemington Racecourse (Platform 1), stopping only at North Melbourne (Platform 3) and Southern Cross (Platform 12), enroute to Flinders Street (Platform 6).

  • From 9:30pm to 11:00pm, services will depart every 30 minutes.
  • From 11:00pm to 11:50pm, services will depart every 10 minutes.
  • The last departure from Flemington Racecourse will be at 11:50pm.

Trains returning from Flinders Street will stop at Southern Cross (Platform 11) and North Melbourne (Platform 4) on approach to Flemington Racecourse.

Getting to and from Flemington Racecourse

Route 57

  • Trams will operate via Route 57 from Stop 01 Flinders Street / Elizabeth Street and passengers can alight at Stop 32 Sandown Road/ Epsom Road via Flemington Road. 

Route 57a

  • Additional shuttle services will operate from 3:30pm to 11:59pm every 30 minutes between Stop 01 Flinders Street / Elizabeth Street and Stop 40 Epsom Road / Maribyrnong Road via Flemington Road.

Metropolitan bus

Getting to and from Flemington Racecourse

The following bus route travels close to Flemington Racecourse: 

  • Route 472 (Williamstown to Moonee Ponds via Footscray)

Passengers can catch buses from Footscray Station and alight at Langs Road/Showgrounds.
