Public transport during the Melbourne Cup Public Holiday

Public transport services will operate to altered timetables, or some services may not be in service. For more information on getting to the event, visit: Melbourne Cup Carnival. 

There are half price fares across Victoria until 1 January 2027. On public holidays daily fare caps apply: 

  • $4 full fare
  • $2 concession and senior 

For more information, visit: Fares - Transport Victoria.

Metropolitan train

  • Metro trains run to a Saturday timetable with extra services.
  • Night Network trains will not run. 

Metropolitan tram

  • Trams run to a Saturday timetable.
  • Night Network trams will not run. 

Regional train and coach

  • V/Line trains run to a Saturday timetable.
  • Coaches will run to an altered timetable. 

Metropolitan and regional bus

  • Metropolitan buses will mostly run to a Saturday timetable, with some exceptions.
  • Regional bus services vary by operator.
  • Night network services will not run.
  • Check timetables, at: Transport Victoria - Timetables.

Plan your journey

  • Plan ahead by using the Journey planner.
