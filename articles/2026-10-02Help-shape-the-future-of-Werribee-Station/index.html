Help shape the future of Werribee Station

We are planning for the future of the Werribee Station precinct and want to hear from the people who use it every day. 

Whether you catch the train or bus, drive, cycle or walk through the area, your feedback will help us understand what's most important to you.

Your feedback will help shape a plan for Werribee Station so it continues to meet the needs of the growing Werribee community.

Your feedback will be used to help inform planning for future improvements at Werribee Station. 

To find out more and have your say visit the Engage Victoria website or come and speak to us at a local pop-up session:

  • Werribee station underpass
    • Wednesday 7 October 2026, 2pm – 3.30pm
  • Woolworths Werribee Central
    • Thursday 15 October 2026, 12pm – 1.30pm
  • Werribee station underpass
    • Thursday 22 October 2026, 2pm – 3.30pm

You can arrive at any time during the pop-up session to chat to a member of the team.

Consultation closes on 25 October, 2026.
