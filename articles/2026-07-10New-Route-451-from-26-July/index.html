New Route 451 from 26 July

From Sunday 26 July 2026, Route 451 Thornhill Park to Cobblebank Station will connect Thornhill Park and Cobblebank residents with Cobblebank Station.

Route 451 will give Thornhill Park residents a fixed route bus service for the first time and give Cobblebank residents another regular bus service, with more ways to get around the local area and connect with trains.

Route 451 will make it easier to get to Central Square Shopping Centre, Cobblebank Village Shopping Centre, Kolorer College and Cobblebank Station.

More than 20 new bus stops are being built as part of the introduction of Route 451.

Buses will run every 20 minutes on weekdays and every 40 minutes on weekends, from early morning until late at night, seven days a week.
