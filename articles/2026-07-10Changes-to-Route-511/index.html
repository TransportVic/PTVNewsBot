Changes to Route 511

To plan your journey:

  • Download the PTV App
  • Use the journey planner
  • Check the Route 511 timetable
  • or call 1800 800 007.

Timetable changes

  • We’re adding 322 new weekly trips to Route 511.
  • Route 511 will run approximately every 40 minutes all week from:
    • 5:30 am to 10:40 pm on weekdays
    • 6.55 am to 10:40 pm on Saturdays
    • 7:35 am to 9:30 pm on Sundays.

Times indicate the departure of the first and last service.

Route changes 

  • Route 511 will travel the full length of Mandalay Circuit, giving residents in the north of Mandalay Estate bus services for the first time.
  • Route 511 trips from Craigieburn Station will travel Mandalay Circuit in an anticlockwise loop. Only these trips travel through Mandalay Circuit.
  • Passengers on Mandalay Circuit heading to Craigieburn Station can board Route 511 while it completes the loop. The bus will stop and wait for 1-5 minutes at Mantel Green/Lithgow Street before continuing to Craigieburn Station.
  • The bus destination display will change from “Mandalay” to “Craigieburn Station” before entering Mandalay Circuit, so passengers know the bus is continuing to Craigieburn Station.
  • Route 511 will not run along Golf Links Drive in Beveridge. Passengers can walk to an alternative Route 511 stop on Mandalay Circuit.
  • Route 511 will be extended to the eastern part of Beveridge once road works are completed in 2027.

Stop changes 

The three bus stops on Golf Links Drive will no longer be serviced by Route 511. 

These stops are: 

  • Pacific Gra/Golf Links Dr (stop ID 51634)
  • Mistral Way/Golf Links Dr (stop ID 27910)
  • Siren St/Golf Links Dr (stop ID 27911).


Passengers should check the new Route 511 map and timetable and plan ahead before travelling.
