Wonthaggi - Leongatha bus operating year-round

On Monday 26 October 2026, the Wonthaggi - Leongatha via Inverloch intertown bus will have a new timetable and operate year-round on weekdays. Currently, services operate on school days only.

To plan your journey:

  • Download the PTV App
  • Use the journey planner
  • Check the Wonthaggi - Leongatha via Inverloch timetable
  • or call 1800 800 007.

What’s changing? 

  • Services will operate year-round on weekdays.
  • Services will continue to run one return trip per day, Monday to Friday.
  • There will be minor timetable changes to better connect services with school bell times, giving students at least 10 minutes to get to and from the bus.

Stop changes 

Five stops along the Bass Highway between Leongatha and Inverloch will close due to low passenger numbers.  

The following stops will close:

  • Outtrim-Inverloch Road/Bass Highway (stop ID 39154)
  • Outtrim-Inverloch Road/Bass Highway (stop ID 39155)
  • Zotti And Dowels Road/Bass Highway (stop ID 37949)
  • Racecourse Road/Bass Highway (stop ID 37948)
  • Koonawarra-Inverloch Rd/Bass Highway (Stop ID 37950).


Passengers should check the new Wonthaggi - Leongatha timetable and plan ahead before travelling.
