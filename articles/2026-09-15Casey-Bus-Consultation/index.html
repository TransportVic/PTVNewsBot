Casey Bus Consultation

We've developed plans to improve bus services across Casey, including Cranbourne, Clyde, Berwick, Botanic Ridge, Junction Village, Tooradin, Warneet and surrounding areas, to support growing communities.

We’re proposing changes to bus routes 795, 796, 836, 846, 888, 897, 899, and introducing new Route 896 connecting Lynbrook Station and Casey Fields via Merinda Park and Cranbourne stations.

The proposed changes would:

  • provide faster, more direct and frequent bus services
  • improve public transport access for more than 130,000 people
  • bring buses to new estates in parts of Cranbourne East and Clyde for the first time
  • make it easier to connect to trains, jobs, education, health services and shopping.

For more information and have your say, visit Engage Victoria.
