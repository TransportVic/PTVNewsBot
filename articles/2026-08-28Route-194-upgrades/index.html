Route 194 upgrades

On 13 September 2026, we’re improving Route 194 by adding more services, extending operating hours and improving coverage in Werribee.

The route will be renamed Route 194 Werribee Station - Wyndham Vale Station via Ison Road.

To plan your journey:

  • Download the PTV App
  • Use the journey planner
  • Check the Route 194 timetable
  • or call 1800 800 007.

Timetable changes

  • We’re adding 122 new weekly trips to Route 194.
  • On weekdays, buses run every 20 minutes from 6 am to 9 pm. Buses run every 40 minutes at other times.
  • On Saturdays and Sundays, buses run every 40 minutes.
  • On weekdays, buses run from 5:20 am to 12:50 am.
  • On Saturdays, buses run from 6:10 am to 12:50 am.
  • On Sundays, buses run from 7:10 am to 11:30 pm.

Times indicate the departure of the first and last service.

Route changes 

  • We’re extending Route 194 to Werribee Station via Kings Leigh and Riverwalk estates.
  • Extending Route 194 will provide more residents in Kings Leigh and Riverwalk estates with public transport for the first time.
  • Extending Route 194 will connect more residents in Kings Leigh and Riverwalk estates to metro trains at Werribee Station and V/Line trains at Wyndham Vale Station.
  • Extending Route 194 will provide residents in Harpley and Mambourin with direct access to Werribee Station.

Passengers should check the new Route 194 map and timetable and plan ahead before travelling.
