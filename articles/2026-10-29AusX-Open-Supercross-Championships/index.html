AusX Open Supercross Championships

Extra public transport services will run for the AusX Open Supercross Championships at GMHBA Stadium on Saturday 7 November 2026.

Service Changes will also apply to local Bus and Coach services on Moorabool Street. 

The event commences at 5pm. 

Please visit the event website for further event information. 

Plan your journey

Plan your journey using Journey Planner and check Disruptions for service changes that may affect your journey. 

 

 

Start travelling

 

 

Metropolitan train 

Getting to and from Melbourne 

Regular trains will operate via Southern Cross Station for passengers wishing to connect to a regional train travelling to and from South Geelong.

Metropolitan tram 

Getting to and from Melbourne 

Trams operate via Flinders Street and Southern Cross Stations, for passengers wishing to connect to a regional train travelling to and from South Geelong.

Regional bus 

Getting to and from GMHBA Stadium 

The following bus services travel via GMHBA Stadium on Moorabool Street:

  • Route 01
  • Route 24
  • Route 41
  • Route 42
  • Route 53
  • Route 55 

Service Changes 

Buses in Geelong will experience service changes due to road closures outside of GMHBA Stadium before and after the match. 

Road closures will be implemented on Moorabool Street from 1.5hours prior to start time until 1 hour post finish time, to facilitate the safe arrival and departure of patrons. 

Bus Routes 1, 24, 41, 42, 53 and 55 will not service selected bus stops on Moorabool, Lonsdale Street and Yarra Street and instead travel via Yarra Street and South Geelong Station in sections. 

The following stops will not be serviced during these times: 

  • Moorabool Street/Balliang Street
  • Moorabool Street/Park Crescent
  • Moorabool Street/Kardinia Park and Ride
  • Kardinia Park and Ride/Moorabool Street
  • Moorabool Street/Kardinia Park
  • Moorabool Street/Lonsdale Street
  • Lonsdale Street/South Geelong Station 

Passengers are advised to use existing bus stops at:

  • Moorabool Street/Kilgour Street
  • Moorabool Street/Maud Street 

Passengers, please note there will be temporary bus stops at: 

  • South Geelong Railway Station/Lonsdale Street (26 Lonsdale Street; in Yarra Street)
  • Yarra Street/Balliang Street (262 Yarra Street, 259 Yarra Street) 

Regional train

Getting to and from GMHBA Stadium

Passengers travelling to and from GMHBA can catch train services travelling via South Geelong. 

Extra trains will operate for passengers to the event. 

  • Departing Southern Cross at 2.58pm to South Geelong 

Extra trains will operate for passengers after the event to Southern Cross.

  • Departing South Geelong at 9.41pm to Southern Cross
  • Departing South Geelong at 9.51pm to Southern Cross
