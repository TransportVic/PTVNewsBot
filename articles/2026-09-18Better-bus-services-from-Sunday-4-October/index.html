Better bus services from Sunday 4 October

From Sunday 4 October 2026, new timetables will start for 36 bus routes across Melbourne.

Some routes will also get extra trips, later services or new weekend services, giving passengers more options during busy school travel times, in the evenings and on weekends.

The changes will better connect buses with train services following the opening of the Metro Tunnel.

To plan your journey:

  • Download the PTV App
  • Use the journey planner
  • or call 1800 800 007.
